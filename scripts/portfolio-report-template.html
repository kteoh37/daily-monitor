Interpretation and updates

Domestic JGB buying and foreign-security sales do not identify the same investors and cannot by themselves establish repatriation. MOF long-term debt includes securities beyond JGBs. Cumulative flows measure transactions, not holdings or valuation gains. MOF full weeks are allocated by week-end date; JSDA uses five calendar months, never five nonmissing observations.

The daily dashboard refreshes at 13:00 and 22:00 UTC. Portfolio charts refresh Thursdays at 10:00 America/New_York (GitHub scheduling is best effort). Every check uses official MOF data and one commit-pinned Haver snapshot; Haver is an upstream snapshot, not a direct vendor refresh. Last successful observation dates remain visible if a component fails.
